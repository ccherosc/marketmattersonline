Your Google Analytics shows visitors. Your GBP shows views. Your ads are getting clicks. But leads? Crickets.

This is one of the most frustrating marketing problems a business owner can have — because it *looks* like things are working. Traffic is up. But nothing's converting.

Here are the 7 most common reasons this happens, and how to fix each one.

---

## 1. Your Offer Isn't Clear

**The symptom:** Visitors land on your site and leave quickly (high bounce rate).

**The problem:** They can't immediately tell what you do, who you do it for, and why they should care. If someone has to read three paragraphs before they understand what you offer, most of them won't.

**The fix:** Your homepage headline should answer in one sentence: "We help [who] do [what] so they can [outcome]." 

Bad: "Welcome to Smith & Associates — Serving the Community Since 1987"  
Good: "Same-Day HVAC Repair for Homeowners in Dallas — Licensed, Insured, 4.9 Stars"

---

## 2. No Clear Next Step

**The symptom:** Traffic, but no form submissions, calls, or clicks on your CTA button.

**The problem:** Your site doesn't tell visitors what to do next. Or there are too many options, so they choose none.

**The fix:** Every page should have one primary call to action. Not five. One. And it should be specific.

Weak: "Contact Us" or "Learn More"  
Strong: "Get Your Free Estimate" or "Schedule a 15-Minute Call"

---

## 3. Your Traffic Doesn't Match Your Offer

**The symptom:** Lots of traffic, zero conversions, and you can't figure out why.

**The problem:** The people coming to your site aren't your buyers. They're researchers, competitors, or people looking for something adjacent to what you offer.

**The fix:** Look at your top traffic keywords in Google Search Console. Are those the keywords your ideal customer would search? Or are you attracting the wrong audience?

A commercial cleaning company ranking for "how to clean grout" is getting traffic, but not from people who want to *hire* a cleaner. They need to rank for "commercial cleaning services near me" or "office cleaning company."

---

## 4. Your Site Is Too Slow

**The symptom:** Traffic is high, but session durations are very short.

**The problem:** Google's data shows that 53% of mobile users abandon a page that takes more than 3 seconds to load. If your site loads in 6 seconds, half your traffic is gone before they see anything.

**The fix:** Run your site through Google PageSpeed Insights. Common fixes:
- Compress images (use WebP format)
- Remove unnecessary plugins or scripts
- Use a faster hosting provider
- Enable caching

---

## 5. You're Missing Social Proof

**The symptom:** People are visiting your site but not contacting you, even though your offer seems clear.

**The problem:** They don't trust you yet. Humans buy from people they trust, and a website with no reviews, no testimonials, no case studies, and no faces is an anonymous stranger asking for money.

**The fix:** Add social proof to your homepage and key service pages:
- 3–5 short testimonials with full names and ideally photos
- Your Google review rating (with a link to your profile)
- Logos of brands you've worked with (B2B)
- A photo of you or your team

People don't trust brands; they trust people.

---

## 6. Your Form Has Too Many Fields

**The symptom:** People start filling out your form but don't complete it.

**The problem:** Friction. Every field you add to a form reduces conversions. Asking for name, email, phone, address, company, project type, budget, timeline, and "how did you hear about us" is asking too many questions to a person who hasn't committed to anything yet.

**The fix:** For a first contact, ask for 3 things maximum: name, email, and one qualifying question ("What's your biggest challenge right now?"). Collect more information on the phone.

---

## 7. Your Site Has No Capture Mechanism

**The symptom:** Visitors come, look around, and leave with no way for you to follow up.

**The problem:** Most people aren't ready to buy the first time they visit your site. Without a way to capture their information, you lose them forever when they close the tab.

**The fix:** Add at least one email capture option to your site:
- A newsletter signup in the header or footer
- A lead magnet offer (free guide, checklist, consultation)
- A popup that triggers after 30 seconds or on exit intent

A visitor who gives you their email is 10x more likely to become a customer than one who doesn't.

---

## Where to Start

Run through this list and identify which one(s) apply to your site. Don't try to fix all seven at once. Pick the one that seems most likely to make the biggest difference and fix it this week.

Usually, the highest-impact fixes are:
1. Clarifying your offer and CTA (fixes 1 and 2)
2. Adding social proof (fix 5)
3. Adding an email capture mechanism (fix 7)

Do those three and you'll likely see a meaningful improvement in your conversion rate within 30 days.
