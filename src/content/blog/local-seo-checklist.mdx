If you run a service business — HVAC, landscaping, legal services, home cleaning, plumbing, consulting — local search is where your customers are finding your competition. This checklist covers everything you need to rank in local search results, including the Google Maps "Local Pack."

Work through this in order. Each section builds on the previous one.

---

## Section 1: Google Business Profile (Highest Priority)

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the single most important local SEO asset you have. Most businesses set it up once and forget it. That's a mistake.

- [ ] **Claim and verify your profile** at business.google.com
- [ ] **Select the right primary category** — be specific (e.g., "HVAC Contractor," not just "Contractor")
- [ ] **Add all relevant secondary categories** — Google allows multiple
- [ ] **Write a keyword-rich business description** (750 characters max — use them)
- [ ] **Add your complete NAP** — Name, Address, Phone. Must match your website exactly
- [ ] **Set accurate business hours** including holiday hours
- [ ] **Upload at least 10 high-quality photos** — exterior, interior, team, work examples
- [ ] **Add your service areas** if you don't have a storefront
- [ ] **List all your services** with descriptions and prices where applicable
- [ ] **Enable messaging** so prospects can text you
- [ ] **Post a GBP update at least once a week** — events, offers, or tips
- [ ] **Respond to every review** within 24–48 hours

---

## Section 2: Review Strategy

Reviews are the #1 local ranking signal and the #1 conversion factor for new customers.

- [ ] **Set a review request system** — ask every satisfied customer, every time
- [ ] **Send a direct review link** (get your GBP short URL from your dashboard)
- [ ] **Text-based review requests outperform email** — use both
- [ ] **Target 4.5+ star average with 50+ reviews** for most competitive markets
- [ ] **Respond to negative reviews** professionally and factually — never emotionally
- [ ] **Flag fake negative reviews** through the GBP dashboard
- [ ] **Don't pay for reviews or incentivize them** — Google penalizes this

---

## Section 3: Your Website

Your website needs to tell Google clearly what you do, where you do it, and for whom.

- [ ] **Include your city/region in your page title and H1** on the homepage
- [ ] **Create a dedicated service area page** for each city you serve
- [ ] **Add your NAP to the footer** of every page — must match GBP exactly
- [ ] **Embed a Google Map** on your contact page
- [ ] **Add LocalBusiness schema markup** (use Google's Structured Data Markup Helper)
- [ ] **Get your site mobile-responsive** — over 60% of local searches are on mobile
- [ ] **Improve page speed** — run through Google PageSpeed Insights
- [ ] **Add a clear phone number** in the header (clickable on mobile)
- [ ] **Create service pages** for each of your main services

---

## Section 4: Local Citations

Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number across the web. Consistency matters.

- [ ] **Audit existing citations** with a tool like BrightLocal or Whitespark
- [ ] **Fix NAP inconsistencies** — even small differences hurt your rankings
- [ ] **Claim your top citations:**
  - Yelp
  - Bing Places
  - Apple Maps
  - Facebook Business Page
  - Better Business Bureau
  - Angi / HomeAdvisor (if applicable)
  - Industry-specific directories
- [ ] **Build at least 20–30 quality citations** in your first 90 days

---

## Section 5: Local Content

Content signals relevance. Creating locally-relevant content tells Google you're active in your area.

- [ ] **Write a "best of" or resource post** targeting your city (e.g., "Best HVAC Maintenance Tips for [City] Winters")
- [ ] **Create a community page** highlighting local partnerships, events, or causes
- [ ] **Publish case studies** from local customers with city mentions
- [ ] **Target "near me" keywords** in your content ("plumber near me" = informational intent)

---

## Section 6: Links

Local links from relevant, local websites signal trust and authority.

- [ ] **Join your local Chamber of Commerce** (they usually link to members)
- [ ] **Get listed in local business associations**
- [ ] **Sponsor local events** for a website mention
- [ ] **Partner with complementary businesses** for cross-links

---

## The 30-Day Quick Win Plan

If you're starting from scratch, focus on this order:

**Week 1:** Verify GBP, complete every field, upload photos  
**Week 2:** Send review requests to your last 20 customers  
**Week 3:** Fix your website's homepage and add service pages  
**Week 4:** Build 10 consistent citations on major directories

Do that and you'll outrank 80% of your local competitors who haven't touched their GBP in years.
